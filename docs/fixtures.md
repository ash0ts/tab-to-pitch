# Score fixture qualification

The shipped studies in `src/player/demo.ts` are original TabToPitch material, dedicated to the public domain under CC0-1.0. **A shape, two meanings** contains four measures, guitar and bass tracks, the simultaneous guitar voicing B2–D3–G3–D4, an E minor triad, individual notes, and a higher G/B voicing. Neither study is an arrangement of a third-party song. The tests also generate short original alphaTex passages locally. No downloaded song files are included.

## Progression lesson

**One progression, many places** is a four-bar Dm7–G7–Cmaj7–Am7 accompaniment lesson in C major. Its chord symbols are authored confirmed facts. The guitar and bass transpose together when opening another major-key version; the source ID carries that tonic. The original shape lesson remains available for note-name and exact-pitch demonstrations.

`src/player/variation.test.ts` covers score cloning, backing-track and unselected-bar preservation, timing and automation, cross-bar ties, capo/string pitch validation, explicit gaps, and rejection of unsupported tracks or partial bars. `tests/progression.spec.ts` covers progression controls, generated notation, preview/source comparison, saving and restoring controls, responsive tonal context, and optional recorded-choice audition. These generated fixtures establish their declared contracts, not preservation of arbitrary imported guitar articulations or real-device sound quality.

The renderer browser test samples dark pixels from the captured label rectangle in a full-page PNG, in addition to checking SVG text and geometry. Integrated app captures use viewport screenshots: Chrome's full-page capture can omit SVG inside the application's scroll container even when ordinary viewport captures show the score. A blank full-page capture is not accepted as visual evidence.

Review regressions additionally cover dense sixteenth-note labels, ghost and natural-harmonic decorations, stable mask bounds, a tied attack whose continuation bends, staccato overlap, and withholding triad analysis for unqualified let-ring/palm-mute/custom-duration timing. These are original alphaTex or source-model fixtures. They do not qualify the corresponding techniques across external Guitar Pro formats. Engine tests also reject intervals that skip an unsupported attack or combine simultaneous voices.

## Qualification still required

The importer accepts alphaTab-supported Guitar Pro formats, but this project has **not qualified an external `.gp3`, `.gp4`, `.gp5`, `.gpx`, or GP7 corpus**. File extensions and upstream parser coverage are not evidence that every musical feature is preserved here. Add independently authored, redistribution-cleared files before claiming those formats are fully qualified.

Remaining cases include multi-voice sustain, tuplets, pickups, grace notes, slides, bends, harmonics, dead notes, percussion, alternate tunings from real files, mid-measure tempo changes, complex repeat jumps, and large scores. Some are conservatively excluded from exercises while their native notation and playback remain available; they still need end-to-end fixtures. Safari, Firefox, iOS audio interruptions, long playback sessions, device latency and acoustic output also remain outside the current Chrome gate.

Keep fixture provenance with each future file: author, creation method, redistribution permission, expected musical facts, parser version and qualification result. Do not add copyrighted song tabs merely because they can be downloaded.

See `patches/README.md` for alphaTab's pinned version, modifications, source distribution and asset notices.

## Original musicianship studies

`src/engine/musicianship.ts` defines twelve two-bar studies (four rhythm, four phrase, four expression). Their melodies, attack/rest grids, dynamic/articulation comparisons and worked examples are original material dedicated to CC0, like the existing demonstration studies. The G7/Cmaj7 accompaniment is a deterministic reconstruction. These examples have no commercial recording alignment. Each practice episode freezes its exact versioned stimulus and grading constraints.
