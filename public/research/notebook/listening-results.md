# Listening teacher: useful tools, unqualified conversation

The frozen `listening-agent-003` campaign tests a teacher operating a musical workspace. Its questions and learner histories are authored. They are not observations of real learners hearing songs.

Forty-eight checkpoints span twelve held-out harmony contexts. Two trials produced 45 hosted replies and 51 local fallbacks. The same campaign retains 144 adaptive episode turns and 24 boundary turns; 24 of those turns stopped at the assistance gate before inference. Two capability probes are separate. The 120B model failed capability testing; its 264 full-suite turns are unexecuted.

The independent 20B reviewer classified 52/60 held-out factual fixtures correctly and detected only 40% of critical unsupported claims. It missed both qualification gates. Conversational ratings are unavailable. A runtime critic approved a reply saying the learner had “just heard” something, although the harness had only prepared an action and had no listener. That accepted error remains in the evidence.
