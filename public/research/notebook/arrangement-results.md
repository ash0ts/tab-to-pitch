# Arrangement agent: historical result

Campaign: arrangement-agent-v2, fresh confirmation, 30 October 2026.

The 20B actor answered 46/64 requests correctly across two trials of 32 requests in eight musical contexts. The deterministic parser answered 24/32. The actor did not pass the 90% request-correctness gate. Accepted output validity and request understanding are separate outcomes.

Retained failures include an unsupported rejection of a valid rootless request and a schema error supplying chord symbol “A9” where quality “9” was required. No claim of learning effectiveness follows from this evaluation.
