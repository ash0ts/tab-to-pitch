# Voicing ranking: recorded-choice agreement

Campaign: voicing-ranking-v1, 30 October 2026. GuitarSet accompaniment annotations supplied 4,317 transitions from 168 recordings, in 18 held-out performer/progression folds. Each test supplied the actual previous shape and the desired next pitch-class multiset.

Recording-macro reciprocal rank: learned 0.6898; movement 0.3976; training shape frequency 0.3863; compact/low position 0.2105; stable hashed ordering 0.0401. Learned top-1 recovery was 54.38%; top-3 80.10%. Candidate coverage was 4,317/4,317.

These results measure agreement with recorded choices under the supplied context. They do not establish comfort, preferred voicings, autonomous progression quality or educational benefit. The standalone import excludes the model weights; the optional research comparison is unavailable there and never grades learners. Deterministic ranking remains the default.
