# Ear Lab: keep a transcription hypothesis separate from truth

The local audio work compares analysis, uncertainty and teaching behavior. Uploaded recordings and stems stay local. Model agreement and learner confirmation do not independently establish what a recording contains.

The reliability study's held-out test includes 185 independently labeled notes in four recordings. Recording-macro Brier score was 0.207 for the learned correctness estimator versus 0.165 for fixed evidence rules; lower is better. The learned candidate was not promoted. This small grouped result does not describe arbitrary commercial recordings or learner mastery.

The teaching smoke run retained 24 authored turns and four model turns. Both 20B and 120B failed qualification; all four model turns fell back. Five actual inference requests include continuations. No qualified independent evaluator supplied conversation-quality ratings.

GuitarSet and GAPS supply recorded guitar; Guitar-TECHS and Slakh include technique and mixture checks. Previously inspected material stays regression/development evidence. Authored examples test behavior, not learning. Dataset-specific provenance and exclusions remain in the repository's Ear Lab report.
