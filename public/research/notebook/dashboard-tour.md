# Private evidence: where to look

All links lead to the existing private TabToPitch research project. Access is controlled by Forge. This application does not publish private scores, recordings or learner histories.

- [Agent Lens](https://forge.coreweave.com/agent-lens/wandb/tab-to-pitch-public-data-research): arrangement and teacher calls, tools, validations, rejected proposals and fallbacks.
- [Weave evaluations](https://forge.coreweave.com/wandb/wandb/tab-to-pitch-public-data-research/weave/evaluations): frozen cases, replay scores and criterion-level failures. Replay latency is not inference latency.
- [W&B Models](https://forge.coreweave.com/wandb/wandb/tab-to-pitch-public-data-research/runs/voicing-ranking-v1): actual voicing-ranking fits and recorded-choice recovery.
- [Dataset lineage](https://forge.coreweave.com/wandb/wandb/tab-to-pitch-public-data-research/runs/voicing-data-v1): GuitarSet cohort, features and artifacts. Follow the artifact's Registry link from the authenticated project; the studio does not infer public permissions or a promotion status.

Historical readback is documented in research/forge/dashboard-tour.md. These links are provided for authorized readers; their rendered pages and permissions were not requalified by this presentation-only change. No production deployment or learning-effectiveness claim is implied.
